docs(adr-097): retract false root-cause claim + record causal-claim discipline

RETRACTION. The first draft of ADR-097 claimed the unversioned :latest image was
the root cause of ~1,170 Sentry events, and that rebuilding with a real SHA
(91e7d37 -> 1feed53) drove them to zero. That causal claim was FALSE.

Timestamps disprove it:
  last /api/voice/niches error   2026-07-14T10:07:25Z
  :latest image rebuilt          2026-07-14T12:00:48Z   (+1.9h)
  first SHA-tagged deploy        2026-07-14T~12:45Z     (+2.6h)

The errors had already stopped ~2.6h before my first deploy. The actual fix was
eb20ee5 ("fix(voice): repair /api/voice/niches 500 from retired
lead_topup_price"), committed 09:57Z and deployed ~10:07Z by a prior session.
The genuine root cause of those 872 events is the already-documented landmine: a
FUNCTION-LEVEL import of a retired symbol that evaded startup gates (prod_check
stayed green at 1102 routes) and failed only per-request. Same for PYTHON-K/N/P
(last fired ~6h before the deploy) and qdrant, whose "fastembed model loaded"
line appears on EVERY boot and proves nothing.

The error was inferential: "errors existed -> I deployed -> errors gone", without
checking whether they had already stopped. Absence of errors is not evidence your
fix worked.

ADR-097's guard is UNCHANGED and still justified, but now rests only on what is
independently verified: ${APP_VERSION:-latest} makes image provenance unknowable,
and 3 of 6 containers (worker_heavy, worker_video, app_staging) were observed
live on :latest with APP_VERSION=latest while app/worker/scheduler ran a real
SHA — real version skew.

- memory/decisions.md: ADR-097 consequence rewritten; retraction recorded inline

- CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md: decision entry corrected + new landmine
