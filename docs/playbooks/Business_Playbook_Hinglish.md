# LeadGen AI — Business Playbook (Hinglish)
### Client pitch + pricing + confidence math — ek hi page pe

---

## 1. Tum BECHTE kya ho (yeh sabse important)

❌ Mat becho: "AI voice agent" / "AI calling software"
✅ Becho: **"Qualified leads, delivered. Aap sirf un logo se baat karo jo sach me interested hain."**

Client ko AI se matlab nahi — usse **ready customers** chahiye. Tum technology nahi, **result** bech rahe ho. (Yeh hamara research-proven gap hai: India me koi AI player "leads delivered" package nahi bechta — sab per-minute bechte hain. Tum outcome bechoge.)

**One-liner pitch:**
> "Hum aapke business ke liye din-raat calls karke sirf qualified leads laate hain — jo log sach me interested hain, budget hai, aur baat karne ko taiyar hain. Aap unhe close karo. Pehle 10 leads FREE — pasand aaye to aage."

---

## 2. PRICING — niche ke hisab se (research-backed)

| Tier | Niche examples | Per qualified lead | Appointment/site-visit | Monthly starter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S** (pehle yahi) | Real Estate, Study Abroad, Home Loans, Solar, Insurance, Coaching | **₹500–2,500** | ₹1,500–7,500 | ₹15,000–25,000 |
| **A** | Interior, Dental/Hair/IVF, Immigration, Used Cars, Recruitment | **₹500–2,500** | ₹1,000–4,000 | ₹12,000–15,000 |
| **B** | B2B suppliers, Travel, Packers, Hotels, CA/Legal | **₹150–800** | ₹500–2,500 | ₹8,000–12,000 |

**3 simple packages (kisi bhi niche ke liye):**
- **Starter** — ₹10,000/mo → ~25-40 qualified leads
- **Growth** — ₹25,000/mo → ~80-120 qualified leads + WhatsApp follow-up
- **Pay-per-lead** — koi monthly nahi, sirf ₹X/qualified lead (risk-free feel — yeh sabse aasaani se bikti hai)

---

## 3. CONFIDENCE MATH (yeh yaad rakho — doobne ka sawaal nahi)

| Cheez | Number |
|---|---|
| Ek call ka cost | ₹0.45/min × ~2 min = **~₹1** |
| Ek qualified lead (8-15 calls) | **~₹10-15 calling cost** |
| Wahi lead client ko bechte ho | **₹300-2,500** |
| **Margin** | **60-95%** |

**Matlab:** ₹15 kharch karke ₹500 kamana. Aur yeh ₹15 bhi **tab** lagta hai jab client onboard ho (uske budget/advance se). Khud ki jeb se kuch nahi.

**Break-even:** sirf **1 client** = ₹10-25K/mo. Tumhari saari free-tier AI cost ₹0 hai. Toh pehla client = pure profit.

## 4. PEHLE KISKO APPROACH KARNA (5-10 din ka plan)

Apne shaher/network me yeh dhundo (phone-heavy, lead-hungry businesses):
1. **Solar installers** — "subsidy ke interested ghar walo ke verified leads"
2. **Real estate brokers** — "site-visit ready buyers"
3. **Coaching institutes** — "admission inquiry walo se same-din baat"
4. **Study abroad consultants** — "budget-qualified students"
5. **Interior designers** — "possession-ready ghar wale"

**Kahan se nikalo:** JustDial, IndiaMART, Google Maps pe in businesses ke numbers — ya apne jaan-pehchaan se shuru.

## 5. SALES SCRIPT (cold approach — WhatsApp ya call)

**Opening (WhatsApp/DM best — free):**
> "Namaste [Name] ji, main [tumhara naam]. Main solar businesses ko qualified leads deta hoon — jo log sach me solar lagwana chahte hain, budget hai, aur baat karne ko taiyar. Pehle 10 leads bilkul FREE — sirf yeh dikhane ke liye ki quality kaisi hai. 2 minute baat kar sakte hain?"

**Jab wo pooche "kaise karte ho?":**
> "Hamara system aapke area ke potential customers ko call karke unhe qualify karta hai — interest, budget, timeline. Sirf ready leads aapko WhatsApp/sheet pe milti hain. Aap bas close karo."

**Objection: "AI hai? wo theek se baat karega?"**
> "Aap khud judge karo — pehle 10 leads free. Quality pasand na aaye, ek rupaya nahi. Risk poora mera, faayda aapka."

**Objection: "kitna mehnga?"**
> "Monthly ₹10,000 se shuru, ya sirf ₹500 per qualified lead — jo aapko comfortable lage. Pehle free trial se shuru karte hain."

**Close:**
> "Toh main aaj hi aapka FREE trial set kar doon? Bas aapke ideal customer ka type bata dijiye — area, budget range."

---

## 6. RISK-FREE FIRST CLIENT (yeh formula confidence deta hai)

1. **"Pehle 10 leads FREE"** — client ka risk zero, tumhara validation
2. Free leads deliver karo → quality dikhe → **trust ban-na**
3. Phir paid: "ab ₹X/lead ya ₹10K/mo se continue karein?"
4. 1 khush client = 2-3 referral (yeh business referral pe chalta hai)

**Goal (30 din):** 1 paying client = ₹10-25K MRR = poori cost cover + profit.
