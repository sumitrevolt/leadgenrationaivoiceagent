## 6. Compliance Do / Don't

**DO:** honest, transparent pricing language · AI-disclosure framing for voice ("AI assistant") · "results" with realistic claims · respect DND/consent in any outreach · keep festival/local tone.

**DON'T:** ❌ promise "free calls" (telephony has real cost) · ❌ frame Marketing+Voice as one headline "bundle" (two separate products) · ❌ guarantee specific revenue/lead numbers · ❌ use competitor logos; names only, factual, "trademarks of owners" · ❌ run unconsented bulk WhatsApp/DM blasts.
