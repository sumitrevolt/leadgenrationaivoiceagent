Quick win/loss guide

✅ Hum jeet-te hain jab…

  • Prospect = Indian local business (solar, gym, clinic, salon…) — unka budget ₹1-25k/mo, USD tools ka entry hi ₹20k-4L/mo hai.
  • Customer ki audience Hindi/Hinglish me phone+WhatsApp pe respond karti hai (email-first US playbook India SMB me fail).
  • Owner ko "AI tool" nahi, result chahiye — humara done-for-you qualified-lead delivery + flat predictable pricing risk unse hata deta hai.
  • Compliance sawal uthe — DLT/TRAI/DND/DPDP humare paas codebase me hai, unke paas FAQ me bhi nahi.
  • Marketing + leads + calling EK jagah chahiye (sab competitors single-slice hain).

❌ Hum haarte hain jab…

  • Prospect US/EU B2B SaaS me outbound karna chahta hai — 11x/Artisan ka contact DB + email infra wahan superior.
  • Team khud custom internal agents banana chahti hai (ops/support automation) — Lindy/Relevance sahi fit.
  • Enterprise ko apna voice stack chahiye dev team ke saath — Retell/Sarvam/Bolna infra-depth jeet jaate.
  • Cold-calling at scale chahiye AAJ — humari DLT pending hai (inbound callback + marketing tiers se ladho).
  • Brand-name trust chahiye (a16z/Benchmark-backed vs bootstrap).
Ek-line positioning: "Wo sab 'AI employee' ka tool bechte hain — hum aapko kaam hua hua dete hain: content bana, post ready, calls hui, sirf qualified lead aapke paas. Tool seekhna nahi, result ginna."
