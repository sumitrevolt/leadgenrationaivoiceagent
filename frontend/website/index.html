FAQ

Common sawaal, honest jawaab

Kya calls bilkul free hain? +
Honest jawaab: AI ka "brain" (samajhna, baat karna, qualify karna) lagभग free chalता hai — woh humne optimize kar diya hai. Lekin real phone ring (telephony/PSTN) ka ek minimal per-minute cost hota hai jo telecom charge karta hai, woh koi bhi platform free nahi kar sakta. Isliye hum "free calls" ka jhootha vaada nahi karte — aap sirf qualified leads ke liye pay karते ho (₹200–500/lead), aur telephony ka chhota cost usme transparently included hota hai.
"Qualified lead" ka matlab kya hai? +
Wahi lead jise AI ne call par budget, need aur buying intent ke basis par verify kiya ho. Random number nahi — ek aisa person jo genuinely interested hai aur aapke offer ke liye fit hai. Aap sirf inhi ke paise dete ho.
AI Hindi me theek se baat kar paata hai? +
Haan. AI voice agent natural Hinglish (Hindi + English mix) aur pure Hindi/English — dono me fluently baat karta hai. Local tone aur niche-specific scripts ke saath, taaki conversation real aur bharosemand lage.
Leads kahan deliver hote hain? +
Aapki pasand par — WhatsApp par instant message, HubSpot CRM me direct entry, ya Google Sheets me row. Real-time, zero manual data entry.
Setup me kitna time lagta hai? +
Zyaadातर niches ke liye 48 ghante ke andar campaign live ho jaata hai. Aap niche, target area aur offer batao — baaki scripts, scraping aur calling hum set kar dete hain.
Mera niche supported hai ya nahi? +
20+ niches pehle se ready hain (solar, dental, real estate, HVAC, interior, study abroad, etc.). Agar aapka niche list me nahi hai, hum custom setup kar dete hain — bas sales se baat karo.
