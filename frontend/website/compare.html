Sawaal-jawaab

Yeh comparison kitna fresh hai?

June 2026 me public pricing pages, review sites (G2/Capterra) aur official announcements se compile kiya gaya. Pricing badal sakti hai — final decision se pehle unki sites pe verify karein.

Marketing aur Voice — ek hi product hai kya?

Nahi, do alag products hain. AI Automated Marketing (₹1,999/mo se) me Advanced tier ke andar voice callback ek feature hai. AI Voice Calling Agent full standalone AI telecaller hai jiska pricing flat monthly hai (niche-band A/B/C, unlimited calls).

"Qualified lead" ka matlab kya hai?

AI call ke baad jo lead "interested" classify hoti hai (interest + intent check ke baad) — wahi aapko hot lead ke roop me milti hai, recording + transcript ke saath. Billing flat monthly hai, calls unlimited.

Aap Exotel use karte ho?

Nahi — hum Exotel use NAHI karte. Hum apne free AI stack (Groq STT · Mistral LLM · EdgeTTS) + India-native Vobiz telephony pe chalte hain, chhote business ke liye ready-made Hinglish qualification agent ke saath. Exotel aur SquadStack enterprise alternatives hain jinka apna voicebot product contact-centers ke liye hai.
