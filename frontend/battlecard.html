Quick win / loss guide

Dhanda (EZO)

✓Win when: they need follow-up — calls, WhatsApp, email outreach, CRM. Dhanda stops at the Google post.
✗Lose when: prospect only wants free GBP posts + review replies and won't pay for more.

AdBanao

✓Win when: "poster ban gaya — ab leads kaun laaega aur call kaun karega?" They give creatives, not customers.
✗Lose when: need is purely daily branded posters in regional langs — their 50L+ library + 4.5M-user brand is strong.

MyOperator

✓Win when: a 5–30 person local business sees the ₹52k base + ₹10–20k AI + ₹20k onboarding bill. Hamara marketing ₹5,999/mo tak, AI voice alag ₹4,999/mo se — phir bhi kaafi sasta.
✗Lose when: large enterprise needs mature IVR, big-brand references, dedicated SLAs.

Vodex.ai

✓Win when: Indian local biz wants Hinglish lead-qual calls that are DLT/140/TRAI-legal. Vodex is English/US-collections-first.
✗Lose when: US/global enterprise collections needing SOC2/ISO/HIPAA-certified audit trails.

GoHighLevel

✓Win when: local owner (not an agency) wants it done-for-them, in Hinglish, INR-billed, India-telephony-legal — no setup labyrinth.
✗Lose when: a marketing agency wants the mature GHL ecosystem, marketplace, snapshots & funnel builder to resell.
Positioning discipline: never trash-talk. Plant the landmine question, let the prospect find the gap. And stay honest about the 3 "~ building" rows — credibility > over-claiming.

Key differentiators

  • ✓Hinglish-native voice with KB-RAG, 42 niche scripts, answering-machine detection, DND fail-closed, post-call qualifier.
  • ✓India-legal by design — the one thing foreign tools (Vodex/GHL) structurally can't claim for domestic calling.

Be honest — our soft spots

  • ✓Branded daily-post feed — logo+naam frame, 3 ready posts/day (AdBanao-style; customer portal + /api/brand).
  • ✓Client content-approval loop — draft → 1-click approve → publish queue (agency-grade; Dhanda me nahi).
  • ✓Lead round-robin — team auto-assign + WA handoff (NeoDove/TeleCRM parity).
  • ✗Live human call-transfer w/ context is gated/building — Advanced tier's biggest missing piece.
  • ✗No formal SOC2/ISO/HIPAA certs yet (we have DPDP + consent ledger) — matters only for enterprise/US deals.
  • ✗No agency marketplace/snapshots ecosystem like GHL — reseller tooling is younger.
  • ✗Brand awareness — we're new vs incumbents with millions of users.

Where they win

  • ›Template scale + brand — 50L+ assets, 4.5M users. We can't out-library them today.
  • ›Regional-language breadth + stickers + jingles for viral WhatsApp sharing.
  • ›Daily branded-post habit baked in — the exact P0 frame-engine gap on our backlog.

Where we win

  • ✓Outcomes, not artwork — we turn a poster's inquiry into a called, qualified, followed-up lead.
  • ✓Whole funnel — GBP, site, email, WhatsApp, voice, CRM, invoices. AdBanao is step one of ten.
  • ✓AI staff auto-runs the campaign; AdBanao needs the owner to design + post manually.
