    Section · 04

    Social & credential connections

    Connect the channels your agent will post from or hand off to. Tokens are encrypted at rest. Revoke any time.

    Available channels
    Three states — never_configured · healthy · needs-reconnect. We never show your tokens.
    How OAuth works here
    1. Click Connect. A short-lived state token protects against CSRF. 2. Provider shows the scopes we need. We never ask for more. 3. Token is stored encrypted (Fernet, key from SOCIAL_TOKEN_KEY). 4. Silent refresh runs 5 min before expiry. Revocation is immediate and stops all background work. 5. Disconnect sends POST /revoke, clears tokens, keeps already-synced data.
